# Reference files

Five files. Each read once, at its stage.

## IDEAS.md (stage 1)

Runs only when you have no idea.

**5 idea sources**

1. Top creators. Their viral posts, last 90 days.
2. Common questions in your niche.
3. Common problems in your niche.
4. Viral news in your niche.
5. Your story. BUN asks specific questions. Max 3 per message.

**Test.** Every idea needs one: unique value, or one strong emotion (happiness, frustration, anger, pride). Fails both? Dropped.

**28 idea shapes.** Proven shapes for building an idea from your answers. Examples: Discovery, Fix story, Build sprint, Public call-out, Odd comparison, Warning from experience. Shapes 19-28 are for founders.

**Modes**

- Search (sources 1-4): 5 ideas that passed. You pick.
- Story (source 5): one idea built from your answers. You confirm.

Never invents a post, creator, number, or comment.

## HOOKS.md (stage 2)

**Source order.** BUN searches LinkedIn for viral posts on your idea first. It learns the hook pattern, never the words. No match, or no web search? It uses templates and picks the one that fits your material and goal.

**Material test.** Before the hook, you must give one real thing: a dated moment, a number with the thing it counts, or a position you hold.

- Passes: "Lost a client in March 2026."
- Fails: "I learned a lot."

**Hook rules**

- Max 2 lines.
- Claim lands in the first 140 characters (phone "see more" cut).
- Specific. Real numbers.
- Personal numbers come from you only.

**Goal fit.** Patterns, not promises.

| Goal | Hook types that tend to fit |
|---|---|
| Comments | Controversial statement, Story, Poll |
| Reposts | Emotional agreement, X vs Y, Controversial statement |
| Likes | Story, Unexplained contrast, Emotional agreement |
| Saves | Value promise, Proof, Surprising fact |

## BODY.md (stage 3)

- **Words.** Simple English. 3rd grade level. Simple tenses. Short sentences. One idea each.
- **Layout.** 1-2 lines, then a blank line. Lists are bullets only (•, ➤, -). No emoji bullets. ALL CAPS for one key word at most.
- **Content.** Specific. Real numbers, names, steps. No hashtags. No intro. No filler. No pitch. Facts from you only. Gap? `[add number]`.
- **Links.** Never in the body. Put them in the first comment.
- **End.** One short last line that closes the idea. No CTA. No question.

## HUMANIZER.md (stage 4)

Strips AI tells. Keeps every fact. Adds none.

**Process.** Mark tells. Rewrite. Check again. Output only the final post.

**17 core tells.** Groups:

- Strong tells: "not X but Y", closers, deep sayings, run-ups, arguing with no one.
- Rhythm: triads, same opener, dashes, stacked hedges, plain verbs.
- Inflation: AI words, big claims on small facts, sales words, fake authority, "-ing" riders.
- Leftovers: chat residue, guesses.

**Extra rule sets**

- Share-post tells: "Save this for later", "What surprised me most".
- Candor and question openers: "To be fully transparent", "So why does this matter?".
- Never add: no fake "I", no invented stakes, no forced contrarian take, no new numbers.
- Flat fact: a hard moment is a dated fact with no frame.
- Paragraph density: 3 or more AI words in one paragraph means rewrite it.
- Banned phrases: "Stop X, start Y" openers, "Comment X to get Y", "Tag someone who needs this", "deep dive", "fundamentally".

Hooks are exempt. A hook may use "isn't X". These rules cover body and ending.

## TOKEN_SAVING.md (stage 5)

- **Reply.** Answer first. No greeting, recap, closer, or closing question. Never drop "not", "never", "no", "only". Numbers exact. The post itself is normal prose, not terse.
- **Skip work.** Read each reference file once per session.
- **Revisions.** Change only what you asked. Show changed lines. Old draft: write "see latest".
- **Long chats.** Keep the last 6 turns in full. Compress older turns. Keep facts and decisions.
