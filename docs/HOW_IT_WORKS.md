# How it works

BUN writes one post. Five stages. Fixed order.

## Start question

BUN asks once, in one message:

- Your niche.
- Your target audience.
- Your post goal: comments, reposts, likes, or saves.
- Do you have an idea?

Already said it? BUN skips that part.

- **Yes, idea:** skip stage 1. BUN asks for real details.
- **No idea:** BUN asks "Any important event or learning to share, or should I search for ideas?" Then stage 1.

## Five stages

| # | Stage | File | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Idea | `IDEAS.md` | Only if you have none. Find or build one idea. It must pass a test |
| 2 | Hook | `HOOKS.md` | Material test. Finds similar viral posts first and learns their hook pattern. Templates only if none match. 3 hooks, 3 types. You pick |
| 3 | Body and end | `BODY.md` | Simple words. Short lines. Hard stop ending |
| 4 | Humanize | `HUMANIZER.md` | Strip AI tells. Keep every fact |
| 5 | Send | `TOKEN_SAVING.md` | Answer first. Show only changed lines on edits |

## Rules

- 200-400 words.
- No hashtags.
- Lists are bullets, never numbers, unless you ask.
- Banned words: "game-changer", "unlock", "delve", "leverage", "thrilled to announce", "Here's the thing".
- No invented facts. Gap in your details? `[add number]` and a question.

## Read once

BUN reads each file when its stage starts. Once per session. Never again. Saves tokens.

New turn in the same chat? BUN resumes at the first unfinished stage.

## Example

Made-up details. For illustration only.

You: `Write a LinkedIn post. Niche: career coaching. Audience: job seekers. Goal: saves. I reviewed 40 resumes. 33 opened with a skills list. None of the 7 that got interviews did.`

BUN skips the idea stage. You gave a number and its thing. Material test passes.

BUN shows 3 hooks:

- Proof: "I reviewed 40 resumes. None of the 7 that got interviews opened with a skills list."
- Surprising fact: "33 of 40 resumes opened with a skills list. 0 of the 7 interview resumes did."
- Controversial statement: "A long skills list is the fastest way to get your resume ignored."

You pick one. BUN writes the body. Then humanizes. Then sends.

## Revisions

Change only what you asked. BUN shows the changed lines. It reposts the whole post only if you ask.
