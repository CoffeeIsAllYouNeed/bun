# BODY

After the hook comes the body. First lines pay off the hook's open loop. No delay.

## Words

- Simple English. 3rd grade level.
- Simple tense: simple present or simple past. No fancy tenses where a simple one works.
- Short, common words. "Use", not "utilize".
- Short sentences. One idea each.

## Layout

- 1-2 lines. Then a blank line. Repeat.
- Lists: always bullets. Never numbers (1. 2. 3.) unless the user asks.
  - Use only these: • (dot), ➤, or -. Mix styles across the post.
  - One style per list.
- No other bullet symbols. No emoji bullets.
- ALL CAPS for one key word or short phrase that needs a push. Never a whole line.

## Content

- Be specific. Real numbers, names, steps. Not "many", "things", "tips", "stuff".
- Shape: bad: "Many pros underestimate their profile." Good: "[N] of [N] profiles had [exact problem]."
- Never use hashtags in the post.
- No introduction. No filler ("In today's competitive landscape"). No pitch.
- Specifics come from the user only. No made-up numbers, names, clients, or results. Gap? Write [add number].

## Links

- Never in the body.
- Tell the user to add the link in the first comment. Reason: LinkedIn tends to show posts with outside links to fewer people.

## End

End the post with a hard stop: one short last line that closes the idea. No CTA. No question.
